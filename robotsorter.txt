Product Specifications
RBS-01 / RBS-01W / RBS-01MW

Model	RBS-01 (Standard model; settings can be changed using buttons on the unit)
RBS-01W (Standard model with added wireless functionality and app compatibility)
RBS-01MW (Enhanced waterproof model; app operation only / High durability thanks to brushless servo motor)
Selection method	Automatic sorting based on the tilt of the weighing pan
Processing time	Approximately 1 second / piece
Distribution direction	4 directions (front left, front right, rear left, rear right) + non-standard (does not work and remains in your possession)
Standard setting	Freely set the weight threshold. Six modes (Modes 1-6) can be saved to the device (retained even when the power is turned off).
weight calibration	Features a calibration function using a 100g weight. A 100g calibration weight is included.
communication	Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
Dedicated app	RBS LINK (Free)
iPhone/iPad (App Store) / Android (Google Play) / Windows
Installation	Freestanding, height adjustable
